In 2025 Helix launched its first mobile app for drivers and shippers, a project led by newly hired Head of Mobile Sofia Ramirez and her small squad of engineers. The app, built on the existing Helix API, enabled real‑time shipment tracking and was immediately integrated into the partnership with WorldMove Logistics, which supplied a network of 200 hubs and brought 30 new mid‑market freight forwarders onto the platform. To focus resources on the mobile platform and the new partnership, the company executed a strategic layoff of 10 % of staff—cutting eight positions across engineering, support, and marketing—communicated in a company‑wide town hall and documented in Notion with detailed severance and transition plans.

Early in the year a data sync failure between BigQuery and the Helix backend caused shipment status delays for a key customer, TransGlobal Freight Solutions. The incident was logged in Jira, the root cause traced to a misconfigured Cloud Storage bucket, and the fix deployed within 90 minutes. A full post‑mortem was recorded in Chorus.ai and shared with the entire organization, and a new “Data Integrity” documentation page was added to Notion. The incident prompted a reversal of the decision to migrate the data warehouse to a new vendor; the team stayed on BigQuery and added additional monitoring via Grafana. Meanwhile, the mobile app team produced a comprehensive developer guide in Notion, a user manual in PDF, and a security compliance report for the Q1 2025 audit, all of which were made available to partners and customers.

By the end of 2025 Helix had solidified its mobile presence, secured a strategic partnership with WorldMove Logistics, and tightened its documentation discipline. The layoffs allowed the company to reallocate 12 new hires—Sofia Ramirez, Ethan Patel, Aisha Khan, and others—to core product initiatives. The lessons from the data incident informed a new data governance framework that will underpin the upcoming AI‑driven routing engine. With the groundwork laid for AI‑driven routing, Helix is poised to launch its next generation engine in 2026.
