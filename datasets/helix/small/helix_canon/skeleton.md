# Founding  
- **2020:** Helix Logistics was founded to solve the lack of real‑time visibility for mid‑market freight forwarders, enabling them to track shipments and optimize routes on a single platform.  
- **Founders**  
  - **Maya Patel** – former product manager at SAP; voice: terse, structured, opinionated.  
  - **Luis Hernandez** – ex‑operations lead at Maersk; voice: long‑form, scrappy, hedged.  

# Year arcs  
- 2020: Launched MVP and secured first pilot with a regional carrier, but faced a near‑miss outage during peak season.  
- 2021: Pivoted from manual spreadsheet integration to API‑first architecture, raising a $2 M seed round.  
- 2022: Experienced a hiring boom, adding 30 engineers and launching Helix Fleet, while a major customer escalated a data‑privacy issue.  
- 2023: Rolled out Helix Analytics, hit $1 M ARR, and endured a critical outage that led to a temporary leadership change.  
- 2024: Introduced Helix API, expanded to 50 customers, and announced a Series A of $8 M.  
- 2025: Launched a mobile app, cut 10% of staff in a strategic layoff, and secured a key partnership with a global logistics provider.  
- 2026: Reached 200 employees, achieved $5 M monthly recurring revenue, and announced a new AI‑driven routing engine.  

# Doc-discipline eras  

### Era 1: Bootstrap Beginnings  
- Date span: 2020-01 to 2021-06  
- Discipline level: low  
- Characterisation: Documentation was ad‑hoc, mostly informal notes in Google Docs and a single shared Trello board. The team relied on verbal hand‑offs and quick email threads to capture requirements.  

### Era 2: Structured Growth  
- Date span: 2021-07 to 2022-12  
- Discipline level: medium  
- Characterisation: The company adopted Confluence for internal wikis and Linear for issue tracking. Process documentation grew into living SOPs, and a quarterly “Product Playbook” was created to standardise feature rollouts.  

### Era 3: Scale‑Ready Standards  
- Date span: 2023-01 to 2023-12  
- Discipline level: high  
- Characterisation: A dedicated technical writer produced API docs in Swagger, a product manager authored a comprehensive “Helix Platform Architecture” whitepaper, and all releases were accompanied by formal release notes and change‑logs in GitHub.  

### Era 4: Agile Refinement  
- Date span: 2024-01 to 2024-12  
- Discipline level: medium  
- Characterisation: Documentation shifted to a living knowledge base in Notion, with a focus on user‑centric guides and community forums. The team introduced a “Documentation Review” process in Jira to keep docs in sync with code.  

### Era 5: Enterprise‑Grade Documentation  
- Date span: 2025-01 to 2026-12  
- Discipline level: high  
- Characterisation: Helix Logistics now publishes a public API portal, a developer SDK, and a compliance certification guide. Documentation is versioned, searchable, and integrated with the product’s data‑warehouse for analytics on doc usage.  

# Product lines  
- **Helix Fleet** – 2021 – A cloud‑based freight management platform that centralises shipment tracking and carrier coordination.  
- **Helix Analytics** – 2023 – Real‑time dashboards and predictive analytics for route optimisation and cost forecasting.  
- **Helix API** – 2024 – A RESTful API that exposes core logistics data for third‑party integrations and custom workflows.
