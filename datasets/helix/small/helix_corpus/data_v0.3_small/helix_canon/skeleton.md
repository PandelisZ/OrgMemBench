**Founding**  
2020: Helix Logistics was founded by Maya Patel, former product manager at FedEx, and Daniel Kim, ex‑software engineer at SAP; Maya writes in a terse, data‑driven style, while Daniel favors long‑form, narrative documentation.

**Year arcs**  
- 2020: Launched the MVP and secured the first 10 freight‑forwarding clients.  
- 2021: Experienced a hiring boom, doubling the engineering team to 30.  
- 2022: Near‑miss outage during peak season forced a complete infrastructure rewrite.  
- 2023: Pivoted from a pure API to a full SaaS platform with a dashboard and AI features.  
- 2024: Launched Helix AI Optimizer, driving a 20% lift in customer retention.  
- 2025: Raised a Series B round of $25 M, appointing a new COO to scale operations.  
- 2026: Overcame a major customer escalation that led to a new compliance framework.

**Doc-discipline eras**  
### Era 1: Bootstrap Beginnings  
- Date span: 2020-01 to 2020-12  
- Discipline level: low  
- Characterisation: Documentation was informal—README files, a handful of Google Docs, and a single Slack channel for knowledge sharing.  

### Era 2: Rapid Expansion  
- Date span: 2021-01 to 2022-06  
- Discipline level: medium  
- Characterisation: Introduced a lightweight wiki and standardized onboarding docs; documentation grew to 15 k lines but remained largely ad‑hoc.  

### Era 3: Process Maturation  
- Date span: 2022-07 to 2023-12  
- Discipline level: high  
- Characterisation: Adopted a full-fledged internal wiki, versioned docs, and a quarterly review cycle; documentation now covered APIs, compliance, and internal processes.  

### Era 4: Scale & Compliance  
- Date span: 2024-01 to 2025-06  
- Discipline level: medium  
- Characterisation: Documentation focused on regulatory compliance and data‑privacy, with a mix of formal SOPs and quick‑reference guides.  

### Era 5: Enterprise Integration  
- Date span: 2025-07 to 2026-12  
- Discipline level: high  
- Characterisation: Integrated external partner docs, created a public API portal, and maintained a living architecture diagram; documentation became a product in its own right.
