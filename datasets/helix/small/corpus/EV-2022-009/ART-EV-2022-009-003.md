# Retrospective: Hybrid API Strategy Clarification — 2022-08-15

**Facilitator:** Arjun Mehta  
**Team:** Engineering & Product

## What happened

Previously we believed that on 2021-06-18 the team had split the API design into two prototypes: a lightweight RESTful endpoint for core shipment status and a GraphQL layer for analytics, with Arjun leading REST and Daniel building GraphQL. As of 2022-07-20, the correct account is that the 2021 disagreement was a miscommunication; we now understand that the team agreed to a hybrid strategy—core CRUD remains RESTful, while complex data retrieval uses GraphQL. This hybrid approach was documented in the Product Playbook and communicated to engineering.

## What went well

- The retrospective surfaced the miscommunication quickly, preventing lingering confusion.  
- Both Arjun and Daniel openly acknowledged the misunderstanding, fostering a culture of transparency.  
- The Product Playbook update ensured everyone had a single source of truth.

## What went badly

- The initial spec lacked clarity on scope, leading to divergent interpretations.  
- Documentation was not shared promptly, so the team operated on outdated assumptions for weeks.  
- The decision to prototype both approaches without a clear evaluation plan caused resource strain.

## Lessons learned

- Clear, scoped API design specs are essential—include explicit decision points.  
- Immediate documentation and sharing of decisions prevent long‑term misalignment.  
- A hybrid strategy can be viable, but requires explicit communication of boundaries and responsibilities.
