## runbook
### 1. Deploying a New Sales Campaign
1. **Create Campaign Record** – In HubSpot, create a new campaign with name, start/end dates, target accounts.  
2. **Set Up Tracking** – Add UTM parameters to all email templates.  
3. **Notify Team** – Post a message in #sales‑campaigns channel.  
4. **Launch** – Send first email blast.  
5. **Monitor** – Check open/click rates daily; update dashboard.

### 2. On‑Call (Sales Support) – 24/7
- **Primary On‑call**: Aisha Patel (CEO) – 8 am–8 pm.  
- **Secondary On‑call**: Luis Hernandez (COO) – 8 pm–8 am.  
- **Escalation**: If issue > 15 min, ping #sales‑oncall‑alert.  
- **Documentation**: Log all incidents in the “Sales Incident Log” spreadsheet.

### 3. Incident Response
1. **Identify** – Sales rep reports issue via Slack.  
2. **Triage** – On‑call checks severity.  
3. **Contain** – If CRM outage, switch to manual pipeline tracking.  
4. **Resolve** – Coordinate with IT (Thomas Nguyen) for root cause.  
5. **Post‑mortem** – Update incident log; add lessons learned.

## process_doc
### Sales Lead Qualification Process
**Last updated: 2021-07-04**  
**Authors:** Omar Farah (QA Engineer) – draft; Aisha Khan (CEO) – review

1. **Initial Contact** – Use LinkedIn Sales Navigator to identify decision‑makers.  
2. **Email Outreach** – Send personalized email with value proposition.  
3. **Follow‑up** – 48 h after first email, send a second email or call.  
4. **Qualification Criteria** –  
   - Budget: ≥ $50k ARR  
   - Authority: C‑suite or VP level  
   - Need: Requires freight‑forwarding SaaS  
   - Timeline: Decision within 3 months  
5. **Score** – Assign 0–5 points; ≥3 = “Qualified”.  
6. **Log** – Record in HubSpot; flag for next‑step.

## how_to / faq
**Q: How do I add a new contact to HubSpot?**  
A: Go to Contacts → Create Contact → Fill in name, email, company, and assign to the appropriate pipeline stage.  

**Q: What is the process for requesting a price change?**  
A: Submit a “Pricing Change Request” form in Confluence; Finance will review and approve within 2 business days.  

**Q: Who do I contact if the CRM is down?**  
A: Ping #sales‑oncall‑alert; Thomas Nguyen will be notified immediately.  

**Q: How do I schedule a demo for a prospect?**  
A: Use the “Schedule Demo” button in HubSpot; it auto‑books a 30‑minute slot in the rep’s calendar.
