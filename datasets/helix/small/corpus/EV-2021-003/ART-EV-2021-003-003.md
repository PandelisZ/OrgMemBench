# Incident: Load Balancer Misconfiguration Outage — INC-2021-05-22-001

**Status:** Resolved  
**Severity:** SEV-2  
**Started:** 2021-05-22 14:00  
**Resolved:** 2021-05-22 16:00  
**Customer impact:** All mid‑market freight‑forwarding clients experienced a 2‑hour service outage; API endpoints returned 503 errors.  
**Incident commander:** Omar Khaled  

## Current understanding

The load balancer was misconfigured during the latest deployment, lacking the required health‑check. This caused the balancer to route traffic to unhealthy instances, leading to a full service outage. The issue was identified by Omar Khaled and Omar Farah, and Arjun Mehta performed an immediate rollback and redeployment of the corrected configuration.

## Timeline (live‑updated)

- 14:00 — Service degradation detected; metrics show 503 errors across API endpoints.

- 14:10 — Arjun Mehta rolls back to previous deployment.  
- 14:15 — Load balancer settings restored to prior configuration.  
- 14:20 — Re‑deployment of corrected configuration initiated.  
- 14:30 — Health checks pass; traffic restored.  
- 15:45 — Monitoring confirms stable operation.  
- 16:00 — Incident marked as resolved; post‑mortem scheduled.

## Mitigation

- Immediate rollback of the last deployment to eliminate the misconfigured load balancer.  
- Restoration of the previous load balancer settings.  
- Redeployment of the corrected configuration with the missing health‑check added.  
- Added a health‑check validation step to the deployment pipeline to prevent recurrence.
