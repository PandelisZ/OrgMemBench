# Incident: Critical Service Outage — INC-2022-05-22-01

**Status:** Investigating  
**Severity:** SEV-3  
**Started:** 2022-05-22 09:12  
**Resolved:** TBD  
**Customer impact:** All mid‑market freight‑forwarding clients unable to access the internal order‑processing API, causing delayed shipment confirmations.  
**Incident commander:** Omar Al‑Zahra  

## Root cause  
A misconfigured firewall rule blocked critical traffic to internal services, causing the outage on 2022-05-22. Initial diagnostics indicate a blockage in the internal firewall, preventing traffic to the order‑processing microservice. The issue appears to stem from a recently deployed rule that inadvertently denied inbound traffic on port 443.

## Decision  
"The investigation determined that a misconfigured firewall rule was the root cause of the 2022-05-22 outage. The rule was corrected, the firewall configuration was revalidated, and the change control" was logged and approved on **2022-06-10**.

## Current understanding  
The firewall rule was inadvertently set to deny inbound HTTPS traffic from the internal subnet. The rule was corrected, the configuration re‑validated, and the change control was approved on 2022-06-10.
