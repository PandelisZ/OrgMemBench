# Data Retention Policy Update (90 days)

> **Last updated:** 2022-04-30  
> **Owner:** Arjun Mehta  
> **Status:** Stale  

## Overview

Just a quick note on the upcoming change to our data retention policy. We’re planning to limit customer logs to 90 days, down from 180 days, to better align with privacy audit findings and regulatory expectations. 🚨  

## Decision

- **Date:** 2022-05-01 (mid‑morning engineering‑lead meeting)  
- **Participants:** Arjun Mehta (P‑0005) & Maya Patel (P‑0001)  
- **Outcome:** Adopt new 90‑day retention policy.  
- **Reason:** Data‑privacy audit highlighted risk of retaining logs beyond 90 days. ⚠️  

## Implementation

- Update Confluence page “Data Retention Policy” (will be superseded by new doc).  
- Configure Snowflake retention rules to purge logs after 90 days.  
- Notify product teams via the quarterly Product Playbook. 📚  

## Communication

FYI, this might help: we’ll send an email blast to all product leads and add a note in the Playbook. Let me know if you need more details on the Snowflake config. 📬  

## Open questions / TODOs

- Schedule a follow‑up review in Q3 to assess impact on analytics.
