In early 2022, Helix Logistics secured a $12 million Series A round led by VentureWave Capital, which allowed the company to grow from 20 to 30 employees. The new capital was used to bring on a VP of Sales, Ethan Ramirez, a Head of Product, Lena Chen, a Senior Backend Engineer, Raj Patel, a Data Engineer, Carlos Ruiz, a Customer Success Manager, Sofia Martinez, a UX Designer, and a handful of additional developers and support staff. The influx of talent coincided with a strategic pivot away from the B2C marketplace that had been a holdover from the 2020 launch, and the company began to re‑brand its core offering as the Helix Freight Suite, targeting mid‑market freight forwarders. At the same time, the company replaced HubSpot with Salesforce for CRM, Linear with Jira for engineering tickets, Otter.ai with Fireflies.ai for meeting transcripts, and Figma with Adobe XD for design, reflecting a shift toward enterprise‑grade tooling.

The pivot was put into action with the launch of the Helix Dashboard in Q2, a real‑time shipment tracking and analytics interface that became the centerpiece of the new product suite. The dashboard’s rollout was driven by a key customer event: TransGlobal Logistics signed a three‑year contract that required a custom integration and a dedicated support channel; the team delivered the integration within two weeks, and the new dashboard was highlighted in the customer’s onboarding materials. The year also saw a technical incident when a misconfigured cron job duplicated shipment records, causing a temporary spike in data volume that triggered a 45‑minute outage of the reporting API.
