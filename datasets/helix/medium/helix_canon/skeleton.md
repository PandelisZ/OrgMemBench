## Founding  
Helix Logistics was founded in 2020 by Maya Patel, former freight operations manager at Maersk, and Daniel Kim, former software engineer at Atlassian, to streamline freight‑forwarding workflows with real‑time visibility; Maya writes in a terse, data‑driven style, while Daniel favors long‑form, narrative documentation.

## Year arcs  
- 2020: Founded and built a minimal viable product, hiring a core team of 5 engineers and securing first pilot with a regional carrier.  
- 2021: Launched Helix API, experienced a near‑miss outage during a high‑volume shipment surge, prompting a shift to a microservices architecture.  
- 2022: Secured Series A funding, expanded to 30 employees, and introduced Helix Dashboard, while a strategic pivot moved focus from B2C to mid‑market freight forwarders.  
- 2023: Faced a critical customer escalation that led to a product redesign, followed by a 15 % layoff to streamline operations and a leadership change with a new COO.  
- 2024: Released Helix AI Optimizer, achieved 20 % cost savings for flagship clients, and onboarded 10 new enterprise customers.  
- 2025: Implemented a company‑wide documentation overhaul, doubling internal wiki usage, and celebrated 85 employees with a global virtual summit.  
- 2026: Planned a Series B round, integrated AI‑driven predictive analytics into the platform, and announced a strategic partnership with a major logistics consortium.

## Doc‑discipline eras  

### Era 1: Bootstrap Beginnings  
- Date span: 2020-01 to 2020-12  
- Discipline level: low  
- Characterisation: Documentation was informal, limited to README files and a handful of Slack threads; the team relied on verbal knowledge transfer and ad‑hoc notes.  

### Era 2: Structured Foundations  
- Date span: 2021-01 to 2021-12  
- Discipline level: medium  
- Characterisation: The company introduced a shared Notion workspace, formalized API specs, and began maintaining a public‑facing product roadmap; documentation grew from 5 pages to 30.  

### Era 3: Enterprise‑grade Documentation  
- Date span: 2022-01 to 2022-12  
- Discipline level: high  
- Characterisation: A dedicated technical writer was hired, a style guide enforced, and all internal and external docs were migrated to Confluence; the team adopted versioned documentation with automated linting.  

### Era 4: Agile Documentation  
- Date span: 2023-01 to 2023-12  
- Discipline level: medium  
- Characterisation: Documentation became tightly coupled with sprint cycles, with daily stand‑up notes captured in Confluence and a lightweight wiki for quick updates; the focus shifted to just‑in‑time docs.  

### Era 5: Knowledge‑centric Culture  
- Date span: 2024-01 to 2026-12  
- Discipline level: high  
- Characterisation: The company built a knowledge graph linking product docs, support tickets, and code repositories; documentation was treated as a first‑class product, with quarterly reviews and a dedicated docs‑as‑code pipeline.  

## Product lines  
- **Helix API** (2021): A RESTful interface that exposes real‑time shipment data and routing analytics to partner systems.  
- **Helix Dashboard** (2022): A web portal that visualizes freight metrics, carrier performance, and cost breakdowns for mid‑market forwarders.  
- **Helix AI Optimizer** (2024): An AI‑driven recommendation engine that suggests optimal routing, carrier selection, and load consolidation to reduce costs.
