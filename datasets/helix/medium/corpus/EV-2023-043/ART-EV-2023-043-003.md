# Incident: Duplicate Records in ETL Pipeline — INC-2023-04-12-01

**Status:** Investigating  
**Severity:** SEV-3  
**Started:** 2023-04-12 09:15  
**Resolved:** TBD  
**Customer impact:** TransLink Logistics reported data integrity violations; their shipment records were duplicated, causing confusion in downstream analytics.  
**Incident commander:** Omar Al‑Jabri  

## Current understanding

The latest deployment introduced a misconfigured data validation rule in the ETL pipeline. This allowed duplicate records to slip through, leading to integrity violations reported by TransLink Logistics. The issue is isolated to the `validate_records` component of the ingestion service.

## Timeline (live‑updated)

- 09:15 — Incident detected by automated monitoring; alert fired for duplicate record spike.  
- 09:18 — Omar Al‑Jabri notified the incident response team.  
- 09:20 — Leila Hassan and Nia Osei began triage; identified the offending validation rule.  
- 09:25 — Decision made to temporarily suspend the ingestion service to prevent further duplicates.  
- 09:30 — Service suspension executed; data flow halted.  
- 09:35 — Rollback of the latest deployment to the previous stable version.  
- 09:40 — Hotfix drafted to correct the validation logic; code pushed to staging.  
- 09:45 — Hotfix deployed to production; data integrity restored.  

## Mitigation

- **Suspended** the ingestion service to stop duplicate ingestion.  
- **Rolled back** the last deployment to revert to known good state.  
- **Isolated** the faulty ETL component (`validate_records`).  
- **Deployed** a hotfix correcting the validation rule.  
- **Monitored** data streams post‑fix; no further duplicates observed.  

## Customer comms

- 09:20 — TransLink Logistics notified via Slack and email that an issue was being investigated.  
- 09:45 — Customer informed that the ingestion service is back online and data integrity has been restored.
