# Incident: Sales portal outage due to load‑balancer mis‑config — INC-2023-11-15-001

**Status:** Identified  
**Severity:** SEV-2  
**Started:** 2023-11-15 10:00

## Current understanding

The sales portal went down at 10:00 UTC because a recent load‑balancer rule was incorrectly applied, causing traffic to be dropped. The rule change was rolled back to the default configuration, restoring routing and bringing the portal back online.
