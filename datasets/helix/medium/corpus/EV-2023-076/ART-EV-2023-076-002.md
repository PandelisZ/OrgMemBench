# Incident: Load Balancer Misconfiguration Dropped Production Traffic — INC-2023-07-20-01

**Status:** Investigating  
**Severity:** SEV-2  
**Started:** 2023-07-20 14:12  
**Resolved:** TBD  
**Customer impact:** API endpoints were unreachable for ~30 minutes, affecting all mid‑market freight‑forwarding customers.  
**Incident commander:** Ethan Brooks

## Current understanding

The production API traffic was being dropped by the load balancer due to an accidental misconfiguration of the routing rules. The issue was identified by the DevOps team after noticing a sudden spike in 5xx errors and a drop in traffic metrics.

## Timeline (live‑updated)

- 14:12 — Incident detected: spike in 5xx errors, traffic drop.  
- 14:15 — Ethan and Victor confirm load balancer misconfiguration.  
- 14:18 — Rollback to previous stable LB configuration.  
- 14:20 — Re‑apply correct routing rules.  
- 14:22 — Health‑check probe added to LB.  
- 14:25 — Alerting rules updated to notify DevOps within seconds of any future misconfigurations.  
- 14:30 — Traffic restored; endpoints responding normally.  

## Mitigation

- Rolled back the load balancer to the last known good configuration.  
- Re‑applied the correct routing rules to ensure traffic is directed to the production cluster.  
- Added a health‑check probe on the LB to detect future misconfigurations early.  
- Updated alerting rules to trigger a DevOps notification within seconds of any LB misconfiguration.

- Email blast to affected customers at 14:32, explaining the outage, cause, and resolution steps.
