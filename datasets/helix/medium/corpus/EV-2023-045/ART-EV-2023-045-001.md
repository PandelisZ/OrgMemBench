# Incident: IAM Misconfiguration Breach — INC-2023-05-15-01

**Status:** Investigating  
**Severity:** SEV-2  
**Started:** 2023-05-15 10:12  
**Resolved:** TBD  
**Customer impact:** Potential exfiltration of customer data from internal service; no confirmed data loss yet.  
**Incident commander:** Victor Chen  

## Current understanding

We have identified a misconfigured IAM role that granted excessive permissions to an internal microservice. Attackers leveraged this to exfiltrate customer data. The breach was detected via anomalous outbound traffic on the data warehouse cluster.

## Timeline (live-updated)

- 10:12 — Anomaly detected in BigQuery logs (high outbound traffic).  
- 10:15 — Victor Chen initiates incident response, revokes compromised IAM role.  
- 10:18 — Critical patch applied to affected service; traffic drops.  
- 10:25 — Forensic analysis begins; data exfiltration scope being assessed.  
- 10:30 — Miguel Torres informs sales team, updates Salesforce with affected accounts.  

## Mitigation

- Revoked the compromised IAM role immediately.  
- Applied a critical patch to the affected service; service restarted successfully.  
- Blocked outbound traffic from the affected microservice at the network layer.  
- Initiated forensic analysis to determine data exfiltration scope.  

## Customer comms

- 10:32 — Sales team notified via Slack; CRM updated with impacted accounts.  
- 10:35 — Internal email sent to all customers: “We are investigating a potential data exfiltration incident. No data loss confirmed yet.”  

## Next update

On resolution or by 12:00 if no resolution by then.
