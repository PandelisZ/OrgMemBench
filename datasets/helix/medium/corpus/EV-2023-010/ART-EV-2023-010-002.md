# Mid‑Market Focus Decision — 2023-01-15

**Attendees:** Maya Patel, Marco Rossi  
**Note‑taker:** Marco Rossi

## Agenda

- Review critical customer escalation
- Re‑evaluate B2C marketplace roadmap
- Allocate resources to core product redesign

## Discussion

- **Maya:** “We’ve got a major escalation from a key freight‑forwarder client. The gaps in our core feature set are causing real pain.”  
- **Marco:** “If we keep pushing the B2C roadmap, we’ll dilute the effort needed to fix those gaps.”  
- **Maya:** “Exactly. The marketplace was a nice idea, but the data shows the core product is where the revenue lives.”  
- **Marco:** “Let’s reallocate the marketplace budget to the redesign. We can still keep an eye on B2C interest, but quarterly check‑ins will be enough.”  
- **Maya:** “Agreed. We’ll set up a simple metric dashboard to monitor B2C interest and revisit if the numbers improve.”

## Decisions

- Helix Logistics will continue to focus exclusively on mid‑market freight forwarders.  
- Resources earmarked for the B2C marketplace will be reallocated to the ongoing product redesign addressing the critical customer escalation.  
- The B2C roadmap will be shelved for the foreseeable future, with quarterly monitoring of market interest and a potential revisit if validation metrics improve.

- [ ] @Maya Patel: Update the product backlog to reflect the new prioritization by 2023‑01‑20  
- [ ] @Marco Rossi: Create a quarterly B2C interest dashboard and share with the team by 2023‑02‑01
