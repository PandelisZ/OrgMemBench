- **Luis:** “Let’s push this to the product team. They’ll need to approve the change.”
- **Nia:** “I’ll draft a ticket in Jira and tag the product team. We’ll also commit a prototype to staging.”

## Decisions

- Add a dashboard widget for live status updates.
- Prototype to be committed to staging in the next sprint.
