# Workforce Alignment Discussion — 2023-08-20

**Attendees:** Aisha Patel (HR Manager), Maya Patel (CTO), Daniel Kim (Engineering Lead), Priya Singh (Support Lead)  
**Note‑taker:** Aisha Patel

## Agenda

- Review current staffing vs. product roadmap
- Discuss potential cost‑cutting measures
- Outline communication strategy for any workforce changes

## Discussion

- **Aisha:** “We need to align our headcount with the revised product roadmap. The current burn rate is unsustainable.”  
- **Maya:** “The recent customer escalation forced us to redesign the platform. That redesign will shift our resource needs.”  
- **Daniel:** “Engineering will require fewer mid‑level developers once the new architecture is in place.”  
- **Priya:** “Support can be streamlined by consolidating some of the tier‑2 roles.”  
- **Aisha:** “Given these points, I propose a 15 % reduction across engineering and support.”  
- **Maya:** “I agree, but we must handle this sensitively.”  
- **Aisha:** “We’ll draft a company‑wide email and schedule an HR meeting to address concerns.”  
- **Priya:** “Let me know if you have concerns about the communication plan.”  
- **Daniel:** “Please review the policy on layoffs and ensure we comply with all legal requirements.”  

## Decisions

- Aisha and Maya approved a 15 % workforce reduction, targeting 12 employees across engineering and support teams.  
- The layoffs will be communicated via a company‑wide email followed by a dedicated HR meeting.
