## Overview
This document outlines the proposed sales commission policy that was adopted during the December 1st strategy meeting. The policy replaces the previous tiered structure with a flat 12 % commission on all sales, regardless of volume or product line. The policy is intended to align sales incentives with the company’s new product focus and simplified operational structure.

## Policy Details
- **Commission Rate:** Flat 12 % on all sales revenue.  
- **Scope:** Applies to all sales teams across all product lines.  
- **Effective Date:** Immediate (effective December 1, 2023).  
- **Documentation:** The policy is fully documented in Confluence for all sales teams.

## Implementation
- **Sales Teams:** All sales personnel must update their commission calculations to reflect the new flat rate.  
- **Systems:** The sales reporting system will be updated to automatically apply the 12 % rate.  
- **Training:** A brief refresher session will be scheduled for the sales department to ensure understanding of the new policy.

## Rationale
The change was triggered by a critical customer escalation that exposed misaligned incentives under the previous tiered structure. The escalation led to a product redesign and a subsequent 15 % layoff to streamline operations. Aligning sales incentives with the new product focus and simplified structure was deemed essential to prevent future conflicts and to support the company’s strategic direction.
