# Incident: Accidental S3 Bucket Exposure — INC-2023-02-28-01

**Status:** Resolved  
**Severity:** SEV-2  
**Started:** 2023-02-28 09:12  
**Resolved:** 2023-02-28 11:05  
**Customer impact:** Customer contact data exposed for ~2 hours; no external notification required.  
**Incident commander:** Omar Al‑Jabri  

## Current understanding

An S3 bucket that stores customer contact data was misconfigured to be publicly readable. The exposure was detected when Ethan Brooks noticed anomalous traffic patterns and reported it immediately. The incident was contained within 2 hours after isolation and patching.

## Timeline (live‑updated)

- **09:12** – Ethan Brooks identifies anomalous S3 access logs and reports to Omar.  
- **09:15** – Omar convenes the incident response team.  
- **09:20** – Team isolates the affected database and revokes compromised IAM credentials.  
- **09:35** – Patch applied to S3 bucket policy to enforce private access.  
- **10:00** – Full audit of access logs initiated.  
- **10:45** – Verification that no further public reads are possible.  
- **11:05** – Incident marked resolved; no external notification required.

## Mitigation

- **Isolation:** The database containing exposed data was taken offline to prevent further reads.  
- **Credential revocation:** All IAM roles with access to the bucket were revoked and rotated.  
- **Policy patch:** Updated the bucket policy to `BlockPublicAcls` and `IgnorePublicAcls` and enabled `BucketPolicyOnly`.  
- **Audit:** Scheduled a comprehensive audit of all S3 buckets and IAM policies.
