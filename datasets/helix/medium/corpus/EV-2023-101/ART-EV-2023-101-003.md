# Real‑Time Shipment Tracking Feature Launch

> **Last updated:** 2023-08-02  
> **Owner:** Emily Zhao  
> **Status:** Stale  

## Overview

- On **1 Aug 2023** we launched the **Real‑Time Shipment Tracking** feature to all customers.  
- The decision was made by **Emily Zhao** and **Diego Alvarez** to go for a full rollout instead of a staged beta, because the recent redesign had made the feature feel stable enough.  
- The push came after a critical customer escalation that made it clear we needed real‑time visibility.

## Launch Details

- Coordinated with the release sprint.  
- Announced via the product channel on Slack.  
- Feature was considered production‑ready after the redesign.

## Impact

- All customers now have instant shipment visibility.  
- Early feedback shows a drop in escalation volume (exact numbers TBD).  
- A dedicated Slack thread was created for real‑time support.

## Next Steps

- Monitor usage and performance metrics in **BigQuery**.  
- Collect customer feedback in the product channel.  
- Plan incremental improvements based on data.

## Open questions / TODOs

- Verify that all API endpoints are fully documented.  
- Ensure monitoring alerts are in place for tracking failures.
