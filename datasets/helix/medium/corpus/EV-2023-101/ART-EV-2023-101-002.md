# Real-Time Tracking Feature Discussion — 2023-07-28

**Attendees:** Emily Zhao, Diego Alvarez, Maya Patel, Rajesh Kumar  
**Note-taker:** Diego Alvarez

## Agenda

- Review customer escalation and impact on roadmap
- Evaluate stability of new tracking UI redesign
- Decide rollout strategy for Real‑Time Shipment Tracking

## Discussion

- **Emily:** “We got that escalation from FreightCo last week. They’re losing revenue because they can’t see where their pallets are in real time.”  
- **Maya:** “The redesign we finished in sprint 15 looks solid. The API latency is under 200 ms and the UI passes our QA checklist.”

- **Emily:** “I’m leaning toward a full rollout. The feature is stable, and the risk of a staged beta is that we’ll delay the fix for months.”

- **Rajesh:** “I’ll coordinate with Ops to ensure the autoscaling policy is in place.”  
- **Diego:** “Let’s commit to a full launch on 1 August. I’ll update the backlog and sync the release sprint.”  

## Decisions

- Launch the Real‑Time Shipment Tracking feature to all customers on 1 August 2023.  
- Adopt a full rollout strategy instead of a staged beta.
