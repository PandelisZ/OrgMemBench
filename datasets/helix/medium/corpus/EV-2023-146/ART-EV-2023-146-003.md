Subject: Strategic Decision on TransLink Freight Tracking

Hi Maya, Diego,

Once upon a time in code, we had a critical escalation from TransLink Logistics. After reviewing the incident logs and customer feedback, we decided to pivot the freight tracking module from its current batch‑update design to a real‑time dashboard that streams location data to customers. The new dashboard will replace the existing module and integrate with the existing API, providing instant visibility and reducing manual follow‑ups.

Let me walk you through the key points:

- **Why**: TransLink was unable to receive timely shipment status updates, causing operational bottlenecks.
- **What**: Real‑time dashboard replacing batch updates.
- **How**: Stream location data via WebSocket to the existing API; front‑end in React with D3 visualizations.

Every line has a story, and this change will prevent future incidents. Please review and let me know if you foresee any blockers.

Best,  
Daniel  
CTO, Helix Logistics
