# Incident: Misconfigured Load‑Balancer Rule — INC-2023-11-15-01

**Status:** Investigating  
**Severity:** SEV-2  
**Started:** 2023-11-15 09:12  
**Resolved:** TBD  
**Customer impact:** Sales portal intermittently unreachable for ~45 min.  
**Incident commander:** Maya Patel  

## Current understanding

The sales portal is down due to a mis‑configured load‑balancer rule that caused traffic to be routed to an inactive backend. Initial diagnostics show that the rule was added during a recent deployment but the change was not validated against the firewall policy. We are rolling back the rule to restore service.

## Mitigation

- Rolled back the recent load‑balancer rule to default routing.  
- Verified backend health checks; all services operational.  
- Updated firewall rule to block mis‑configured traffic patterns.

### Retrospective Note (as of 2024-06-15)

Previously we believed that the 2023‑11‑15 outage was caused solely by a mis‑configured load‑balancer rule, and that the rollback restored service within 45 minutes. We now understand that the root cause was a mis‑configured firewall rule that was not captured in the original documentation. Maya Patel and the CTO reviewed the audit findings and decided to correct the incident documentation, update the knowledge graph entry for the 2023‑11‑15 outage, and revise the firewall rule change process to include automated validation. They also added a new monitoring rule to detect misconfigurations before they affect the sales portal.
