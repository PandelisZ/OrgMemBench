# Incident: Load Balancer Misrouting Causes Order Service Outage — INC-2023-05-05-001

**Status:** Identified  
**Severity:** SEV-2  
**Started:** 2023-05-05 09:12  
**Resolved:** 2023-05-05 09:45  
**Customer impact:** Order processing service was unreachable for ~15 min, causing delayed order confirmations for a subset of mid‑market freight clients.  
**Incident commander:** Ethan Brooks

## Current understanding

The load balancer was pushing traffic to a backend instance that had been decommissioned. This misrouting left the order processing service offline. The issue was detected by the automated health‑check dashboard and confirmed by a quick manual request to the service endpoint.

## Timeline (live‑updated)

- 09:12 — Alert triggered: `order-service` health‑check failed.  
- 09:15 — Ethan identifies misconfigured routing rule in the load balancer.  
- 09:18 — Daniel Kim approves rollback to last stable LB config.  
- 09:20 — Ethan deploys rollback; “Deploying now” message sent to ops channel.  
- 09:25 — Traffic resumes to healthy instances; health checks green.  
- 09:30 — System uptime restored to 99.9% (monitoring shows no further failures).  
- 09:45 — Incident marked resolved; “Thanks for the support” sent to team.

## Mitigation

- Rolled back load balancer to the previous stable configuration.  
- Applied a hotfix to the routing rule to prevent accidental re‑deployment of the incorrect rule.  
- Monitored traffic and health checks for 30 min to confirm stability.  
- “Deploying now” message confirmed the rollback was successful.

## Customer comms

- 09:27 — Notified affected clients via email and in‑app banner that the order service was back online.  
- 09:35 — Follow‑up message with brief explanation and apology.

## Next update

On resolution (09:45).
