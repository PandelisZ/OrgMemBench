# ADR-2023-150: API Service Level Agreement Revision to 99.9% Uptime

## Status
Accepted

## Date
2023-11-15

## Authors
Daniel Kim, Omar Khalil

## Context
In the grand tapestry of systems, the Helix API had been delivering freight‑forwarding data to our mid‑market partners with a 99.5 % uptime guarantee. A recent escalation from a key partner revealed intermittent downtime that exceeded this threshold, eroding confidence in our reliability promise. The incident was logged in Customer Ticket #CUST-2023-987 and sparked a Slack discussion (#api-sla-discussion) that highlighted the need for a stronger commitment. Prior ADR‑2023‑045 outlined our monitoring strategy but did not address the SLA level itself. The decision to revise the SLA was driven by the imperative to maintain partner trust and to align our contractual guarantees with the actual performance we can reliably deliver.

## Decision
Once upon a time in code, we decided to elevate the API Service Level Agreement to a 99.9 % uptime target. Let me walk you through the specifics: the new SLA will be embedded in the API documentation, and a formal communication will be sent to all partners via email and Confluence. Implementation will occur in the next release cycle, with a rollback plan that triggers if critical incidents exceed 0.1 % downtime. This threshold balances the need for resilience with operational practicality. We will also enhance our monitoring dashboards in BigQuery to provide real‑time visibility into uptime metrics, ensuring that any deviation from the target is detected and addressed promptly. 🚀

## Alternatives Considered

### Keep Existing 99.5 % SLA
We considered maintaining the status quo, but the customer escalation demonstrated that this level no longer met partner expectations, risking churn.

### Increase to 99.9 % SLA with No Rollback Plan
An alternative was to adopt the higher target without a rollback mechanism. We rejected this because it would expose the organization to unmitigated risk if the new target proved unattainable.

### Increase to 99.9 % SLA with Stricter Rollback Threshold
We also evaluated a rollback threshold of 0.05 % downtime. While more conservative, it would have delayed recovery and potentially amplified partner dissatisfaction.

## Consequences
Every line has a story: the new SLA will strengthen partner confidence and reduce escalation frequency. However, it will also increase monitoring overhead and require tighter incident response coordination. The rollback plan introduces a safety net but may trigger more frequent releases if the threshold is breached, impacting release cadence. Documentation updates will necessitate coordination between the API team and the partner enablement group.
