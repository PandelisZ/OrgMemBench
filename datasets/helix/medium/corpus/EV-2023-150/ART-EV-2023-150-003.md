## Overview  
Once upon a time in code, a partner reached out with a legitimate concern: intermittent API downtime had slipped past our 99.5 % uptime guarantee. This incident highlighted a gap between our promise and reality, prompting a swift review of our SLA.

## Decision & Rationale  
In the grand tapestry of systems, Daniel Kim and Omar Khalil decided to elevate the API SLA to 99.9 % uptime. The new target replaces the prior 99.5 % figure and is a direct response to the recent escalation. Every line has a story, and this story is about restoring confidence and ensuring our partners feel secure in our service.

## Implementation Plan  
From the trenches of development, we will embed the new SLA in the next release cycle. A rollback plan is in place: if critical incidents exceed 0.1 % downtime, we will revert to the previous configuration. The change will be reflected in the API documentation and the Confluence space, with clear rollback procedures outlined.

## Communication & Documentation  
We will notify all partners via email and publish the revised SLA on Confluence. The API docs will be updated to include the new uptime target, rollback thresholds, and monitoring expectations. A brief FAQ will accompany the release notes to address common partner questions.

## Impact & Monitoring  
Monitoring dashboards will be adjusted to track the 99.9 % metric. Any deviation beyond the rollback threshold will trigger alerts to the incident response team and prompt an immediate review. This proactive stance ensures we catch issues before they impact partners.

## Open questions / TODOs  
- Confirm rollback thresholds with the Ops team.  
- Schedule a knowledge‑share session for partner support staff.  
- Validate the updated SLA in a staging environment before production release.
