# Workforce Alignment Review — 2023-04-15

**Attendees:**  
- Fatima Hassan (Finance Manager)  
- Sofia Martinez (HR Coordinator)  
- Alex Chen (Product Lead)  
- Maya Patel (Operations Manager)  

**Note‑taker:** Fatima Hassan  

## Agenda
- Review latest financial projections  
- Discuss operational bottlenecks post‑product redesign  
- Evaluate headcount alignment with revenue forecast  
- Consider interim cost‑control measures  

## Discussion
- **Fatima:** “We’ve seen a 15 % increase in operating costs since the redesign. The latest projections show a margin squeeze of 8 % if we keep the current headcount.”  
- **Sofia:** “From HR’s side, we’ve identified 13 roles that overlap across departments. Removing them would free up 12 % of the payroll budget.”  
- **Alex:** “The redesign was driven by a critical customer escalation. We need to keep the core product team intact to avoid further delays.”  
- **Maya:** “Operations can absorb some of the workload if we re‑allocate resources temporarily. A hiring freeze would give us time to stabilize the new cost structure.”  
- **Fatima:** “Given the financial pressure, a 15 % workforce reduction seems necessary. We should implement it over the next quarter to minimize disruption.”  
- **Sofia:** “Agreed. We’ll draft a transition plan and communicate with the affected employees early.”  

## Decisions
- Approve a 15 % workforce reduction, affecting 13 employees across all departments.  
- Implement the reduction over the next quarter.  
- Enforce a hiring freeze until the new cost structure is stabilized.
