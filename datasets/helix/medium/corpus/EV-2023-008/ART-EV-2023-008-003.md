Dear Team,

After careful analysis of our recent product redesign and the 15 % workforce adjustment, we have decided to replace the flat commission model with a tiered incentive structure. The new policy is as follows:

- 5 % commission for shipments up to 10,000 units  
- 7 % commission for shipments 10,001–50,000 units  
- 10 % commission for shipments above 50,000 units  
- Additional 2 % bonus for on‑time deliveries

All sales partners will see the updated terms in Confluence and via email. The change takes effect immediately. Data speaks louder than words; metrics drive decisions.

Best regards,  
Maya Patel  
CEO, Helix Logistics
