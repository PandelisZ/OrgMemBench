# SLA Review Meeting — 2023-09-04

**Attendees:** Daniel Kim, Miguel Torres, Sarah Lee (Product Manager), Raj Patel (Engineering Lead)  
**Note-taker:** Miguel Torres

## Agenda

- Review current API SLA performance metrics  
- Discuss customer escalation feedback  
- Propose SLA adjustments

## Discussion

- **Daniel:** “We’ve had a couple of 2‑hour outages last month that pushed us over the 99.9% threshold. The freight‑forwarding partners are getting nervous.”  
- **Sarah:** “The escalation from FreightCo highlighted that the 99.9% target feels too low for their operations. They’re asking for a tighter guarantee.”  
- **Raj:** “From an engineering standpoint, we can hit 99.95% with the new load‑balancing rollout. The cost of a 5% credit per hour is manageable if we keep outages under 1 hour.”  
- **Miguel:** “If we bump the target to 99.95% and add a penalty clause, we’ll align the promise with their expectations and keep the partnership strong. I’ll draft the updated SLA text and circulate it next week.”

## Decisions

- Increase API uptime target to **99.95%**.  
- Add a **5% credit** on the monthly bill for each **1‑hour** outage beyond the threshold.
