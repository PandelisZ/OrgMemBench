# Helix Product Strategy Sync — 2023-10-20

**Attendees:** Marco Rossi, Daniel Kim, Priya Patel, Luis Gomez  
**Note-taker:** Daniel Kim (CTO)

## Agenda

- Review status of B2C marketplace initiative
- Evaluate resource allocation and strategic fit
- Decide next steps for roadmap and backlog

## Discussion

- **Marco Rossi** opened with a quick recap: “We’ve been staring at the B2C numbers for months, and the traction is still… *meh*.”  
- **Daniel Kim** acknowledged the lack of momentum: “Every line has a story, but this one isn’t selling.”  
- **Priya Patel** highlighted the opportunity cost: “From the trenches of development, we’re seeing core freight features lagging because of the marketplace devs.”  
- **Luis Gomez** suggested reallocating the team: “Let me walk you through a rough plan—shift the 4 devs to the freight‑core backlog, free up the PM for feature prioritization.”  
- **Marco** agreed, noting the strategic focus: “In the grand tapestry of systems, our core mid‑market freight‑forwarding is where the real value lies.”  
- **Daniel** emphasized documentation: “Once upon a time in code, we’ll log this cancellation in Confluence and set a follow‑up review for the next sprint.”

## Decisions

- Cancel the abandoned B2C marketplace roadmap and remove it from the product backlog.  
- Reallocate the resources earmarked for the marketplace to the core freight‑forwarding product line.  
- Document the cancellation in Confluence and schedule a follow‑up review in the next sprint to ensure no residual work remains.
