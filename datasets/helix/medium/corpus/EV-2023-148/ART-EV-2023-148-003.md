# Incident: Delayed Shipment Notifications — INC-2023-10-18-001

**Status:** Investigating  
**Severity:** SEV-2  
**Started:** 2023-10-18 14:32  
**Resolved:** TBD  
**Customer impact:** TransLink Logistics is receiving shipment notifications up to 48 hours late, causing order‑tracking confusion and customer complaints.  
**Incident commander:** Maya Patel (Product Lead)

## Decision

Maya Patel convened an urgent cross‑functional task force to address the delayed shipment notifications reported by TransLink Logistics. The team agreed to redesign the notification pipeline, with Ni (Nia Osei) leading the redesign effort.

## Current understanding

The notification service is experiencing a latency spike due to a bug in the message queue consumer. Initial logs show a high number of `MessageAckTimeout` errors, indicating that messages are not being acknowledged within the expected window. This is causing downstream consumers to retry and ultimately delay the notification dispatch.

## Timeline (live‑updated)

- 14:32 — Incident detected by monitoring alerts on the notification service latency.  
- 14:35 — Maya convened a cross‑functional task force (Nia Osei, DevOps, Backend, P-0042).  
- 14:37 — Nia Osei started drafting a communication plan for TransLink Logistics.  
- 14:40 — Backend team identified the consumer timeout bug in the `notification_queue` consumer.  
- 14:45 — DevOps rolled back the last deployment of the consumer to the previous stable version.  
- 14:50 — Monitoring shows latency dropping but still above SLA.  

## Mitigation

- Rolled back the consumer to the last stable release.  
- Increased consumer timeout threshold temporarily to 30 s to reduce retries.  
- Restarted the consumer service to clear stale connections.  
- Updated the notification pipeline to use a new queue consumer implementation (pending code review).  

## Customer comms

- 14:38 — Nia Osei sent an email to TransLink Logistics explaining the delay and assuring a fix within 2 weeks.  
- 14:39 — TransLink Logistics acknowledged receipt of the notification.
