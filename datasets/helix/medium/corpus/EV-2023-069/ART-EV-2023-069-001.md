# SLA Revision Meeting — 2023-05-15

**Attendees:** Thomas Green, Fatima Hassan, Alex Martinez (Product Lead), Sarah Lee (Finance), Emily Chen (Legal Ops)  
**Note-taker:** Thomas Green

## Agenda

- Review customer escalation impact on current SLA
- Discuss proposed uptime guarantee increase
- Define penalty structure for downtime
- Approve final SLA wording and rollout plan

## Discussion

- **Thomas Green:** “We’ve received a critical escalation from a key client that highlighted gaps in our 99.9 % guarantee. The product redesign has changed our reliability baseline, so we need to align the SLA accordingly.”  
- **Fatima Hassan:** “I’ve drafted an amendment that raises the uptime guarantee to 99.99 % and introduces a penalty of $500 per 0.01 % of downtime per month.”  
- **Alex Martinez:** “From a product perspective, the new architecture should comfortably support 99.99 %. We’ll need to monitor the new metrics closely.”  
- **Sarah Lee:** “The finance team has reviewed the penalty clause and confirms it’s within our risk appetite. We’ll need to update the billing system to reflect the new terms.”  
- **Emily Chen:** “Legal has vetted the language. The clause is enforceable and aligns with industry standards. We’ll post the updated document to Confluence on June 1.”  
- **Thomas Green:** “Please review the contract. Let me know if you have questions.”

## Decisions

- The SLA will be revised to a 99.99 % uptime guarantee effective 2023‑06‑01.  
- A penalty of $500 per 0.01 % of downtime per month will be added.  
- The updated SLA document will be posted to the company Confluence SLA wiki on 2023‑06‑01.
