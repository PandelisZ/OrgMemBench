# SLA Policy Update – 99.99% Uptime Guarantee

> **Last updated:** 2023-05-15  
> **Owner:** Thomas Green (Legal Counsel)  
> **Status:** Stale  

---

## Background

On 2023-05-15, a critical customer escalation highlighted significant gaps in Helix Logistics’ existing uptime guarantee of 99.9 %. The incident prompted an immediate review of our Service Level Agreement (SLA) to ensure alignment with the newly redesigned system’s reliability and to mitigate future risk.

## Decision Summary (EV-2023-069)

- **Uptime Guarantee:** Raised from **99.9 %** to **99.99 %**.  
- **Penalty Clause:** Introduced a penalty of **$500 per 0.01 % of downtime per month**.  
- **Approval:** The revised SLA was approved by the Legal and Finance teams.  
- **Effective Date:** 2023-06-01.  
- **Documentation:** Updated SLA will be posted to the company Confluence SLA wiki.
