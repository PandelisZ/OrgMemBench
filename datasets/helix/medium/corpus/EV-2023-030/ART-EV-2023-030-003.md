# SLA Policy Review Meeting — 2023-02-20

**Attendees:** Ethan Brooks, Maya Patel, Rajesh Kumar, Lisa Chen  
**Note-taker:** Ethan Brooks

## Agenda

- Review current uptime SLA
- Discuss recent customer escalation
- Propose new SLA target and monitoring approach
- Next steps for rollout

## Discussion

- **Ethan:** “We’ve hit a 99.5% target and the last incident exposed gaps. We need a higher guarantee.”  
- **Maya:** “I agree. A 99.9% SLA would reassure our clients. We should also tighten alerting.”  
- **Rajesh:** “What about the monitoring stack? A 5‑minute threshold sounds reasonable.”  
- **Lisa:** “We’ll need to document everything in Confluence so the whole team can see the changes.”  
- **Ethan:** “Let’s roll this out next sprint and review after a month. We’ll track the impact on incident response.”  
- **Maya:** “Sounds good. I’ll draft the Confluence page and set up the monitoring tiers.”  
- **Ethan:** “Deploying now. Let me know if you spot any issues. Thanks for the support.”

## Decisions

- Adopt a new SLA guaranteeing 99.9% uptime, replacing the 99.5% target.  
- Implement a tiered monitoring system with a 5‑minute alert threshold.  
- Document the policy in Confluence.  
- Begin rollout in the next sprint, with a review scheduled after the first month of deployment.
