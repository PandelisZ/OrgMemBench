# Bulk Shipping Feature Discussion — 2023-03-12

**Attendees:** Luis Ramirez, Marco Rossi, Sarah Patel (Product Owner), Tom Nguyen (Sales Lead)  
**Note-taker:** Marco Rossi

## Agenda

- Review bulk shipping request from high‑value client  
- Assess impact on UI/UX and overall product roadmap  
- Decide on sprint allocation

## Discussion

- **Luis:** “We have a client that needs to ship in bulk. It’s a big deal for them, and they’re willing to pay a premium.”  
- **Sarah:** “That’s great, but we need to consider how many of our users actually need this. Most of our traffic is standard shipments.”  
- **Marco:** “Adding a bulk flow would complicate the UI. We risk cluttering the experience for the majority of our users.”  
- **Tom:** “From a sales perspective, the client’s request could open a new revenue stream, but we should weigh that against the effort required.”  
- **Luis:** “I understand the risk, but the client’s urgency is high. We could prototype a lightweight version.”  
- **Marco:** “A lightweight prototype might still overload the UI. I’d prefer to keep the focus on the core flow for now.”  
- **Sarah:** “Let’s defer the bulk feature to the next sprint. We can revisit it after the upcoming release 2.0 review.”  
- **Luis:** “Agreed. We’ll document it in the sprint backlog and inform sales and design.”  
- **Marco:** “Here’s the mockup for the standard flow. Let me know your thoughts.”  
- **Sarah:** “Thanks for the feedback, Marco.”

## Decisions

- Bulk shipment feature will be deferred to the next sprint and slated for release 2.0 after a joint review with the product owner.  
- Decision documented in the sprint backlog and communicated to sales and design teams.

## Action items

- [ ] @Sarah Patel: Schedule joint review with product owner for release 2.0 — by 2023-03-20
