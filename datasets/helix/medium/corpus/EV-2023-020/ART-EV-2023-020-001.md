# Incident: Load Balancer Misconfiguration Outage — INC-2023-02-15-01

**Status:** Investigating  
**Severity:** SEV-2  
**Started:** 2023-02-15 09:12  
**Resolved:** TBD  
**Customer impact:** 30‑minute outage of the customer portal, all users unable to log in or view shipment status.  
**Incident commander:** Ethan Brooks

## Current understanding

The load balancer was updated with a new routing rule that mistakenly pointed traffic to a backend pool that was offline. This caused the customer portal to return 503 errors for all requests. The misconfiguration was introduced during a routine deployment of the LB rules on 2023‑02‑15 08:45.

## Timeline (live‑updated)

- 09:12 — Incident detected by monitoring alerts (HTTP 503 spike).  
- 09:15 — Ethan and Emily Zhao notified; Nia Osei joins the incident channel.  
- 09:18 — Confirmed LB rule pointing to unavailable backend.  
- 09:20 — Rolling back LB configuration to previous stable state.  
- 09:22 — Hotfix deployed to correct routing rule syntax.  
- 09:25 — Portal traffic restored; monitoring shows 200 OK responses.  

## Mitigation

- Rolled back the load balancer configuration to the last known good state.  
- Applied a hotfix that corrects the mis‑routing rule and validates the backend pool status before activation.  
- Updated alerting thresholds to flag any future LB rule changes that target offline pools.  

Deploying now; the portal should be fully functional by 09:30.  

## Customer comms

- 09:20 — Sent a status update to the customer portal support page: “We’re experiencing a brief outage due to a load balancer issue. Our team is working on a fix.”  
- 09:25 — Notified all customers via email that the portal is back online.
