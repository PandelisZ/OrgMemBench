- Assess current tracking module limitations

## Discussion

- **Nia Osei** opened with the escalation details: “We lost a 48‑hour SLA on a TransLink shipment because the tracking data was out of sync.” She highlighted usability pain points and data accuracy gaps.
- **Maya Patel** emphasized the risk to customer retention: “If we don’t fix this, we’ll lose more mid‑market accounts.”
- **Diego Alvarez** explained the technical constraints: “Our current monolith can’t ingest real‑time updates without breaking other services.” He suggested an API‑first, modular architecture to decouple tracking from the core system.
- Maya asked about sprint capacity: “Can we fit this into a 12‑week cycle?”  
- Diego confirmed: “Yes, with a dedicated cross‑functional team and clear backlog grooming.”

## Decisions

- Initiate a full redesign of the shipment tracking module.
- Adopt a modular, API‑first architecture to support real‑time visibility and data accuracy.
- Set a 12‑week sprint cycle and allocate a dedicated cross‑functional team.
