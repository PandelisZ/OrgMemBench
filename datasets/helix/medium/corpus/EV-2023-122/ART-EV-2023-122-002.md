# Mid‑Market Focus Decision – 2023‑10‑05

**Attendees:** Maya Patel, Luis Ramirez, Miguel Torres  
**Note‑taker:** Luis Ramirez

## Agenda
- Review recent customer escalation impact
- Evaluate B2C marketplace roadmap vs. mid‑market deepening
- Allocate resources for upcoming product redesign

## Discussion
- **Luis:** “We’ve seen the escalation from our top freight‑forwarding client; it’s clear the B2C plan would stretch our team thin.”
- **Maya:** “If we go B2C, we’ll lose focus on the features our current clients need. The redesign is already behind schedule.”
- **Miguel:** “The data shows our mid‑market accounts generate 70 % of revenue. Deepening those features will keep them happy and reduce churn.”
- **Luis:** “Also, the B2C roadmap would require new integrations we haven’t scoped yet. That’s a risk we can’t afford right now.”
- **Maya:** “Let’s prioritize the redesign and keep the B2C idea on the back burner until we’ve stabilized the core product.”

## Decisions
- Helix will continue to focus exclusively on mid‑market freight forwarders.
- The B2C marketplace roadmap is rejected for the current cycle.
- Resources will be reallocated to the upcoming product redesign addressing the critical customer escalation.

- [ ] @Miguel Torres: Draft a communication plan for mid‑market clients – by 2023‑10‑15  
- [ ] @Maya Patel: Coordinate with engineering on redesign timeline – by 2023‑10‑20
