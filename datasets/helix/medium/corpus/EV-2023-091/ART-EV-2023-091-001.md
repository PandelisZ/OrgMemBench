# Incident: Load Balancer Misconfiguration — INC-2023-07-12-01

**Status:** Investigating  
**Severity:** SEV-2  
**Started:** 2023-07-12 14:03  
**Resolved:** TBD  
**Customer impact:** 150 orders dropped, delayed fulfillment for 12 customers.  
**Incident commander:** Ethan Brooks  

## Current understanding

The load balancer health checks were disabled, causing traffic to be routed to unhealthy instances. This resulted in 150 orders being dropped during the 30‑minute outage window. The issue was identified by monitoring alerts and confirmed by a quick check of the LB configuration.

- 14:03 — Alert: `LB health checks disabled`  
- 14:05 — Ethan and Emily review LB config, confirm misconfiguration  
- 14:07 — Rollback to previous stable LB configuration  
- 14:10 — Apply hotfix: enable health checks, set threshold to 3 consecutive failures  
- 14:12 — Traffic stabilizes, order processing resumes  
- 14:15 — Update Confluence quick‑start guide with new LB health‑check steps  
- 14:18 — Add monitoring rule for LB health‑check status  

## Mitigation

- Rolled back to the last known good LB configuration.  
- Applied a hotfix to re‑enable health checks and tighten thresholds.  
- Monitored traffic and order queue; no further drops observed.  
- Updated documentation and added a new alert rule to catch similar misconfigurations early.
