# Incident: Shipment‑Tracking ETA Bug — INC-2023-10-01-001

**Status:** Investigating

**Customer impact:** TransLink Logistics received incorrect ETA data, leading to shipment delays and customer dissatisfaction.  
**Incident commander:** Omar Al‑Jabri  

## Current understanding

The ETA calculation logic in the shipment‑tracking module has produced erroneous values for TransLink Logistics. Initial diagnostics point to a division‑by‑zero error in the `calculate_eta` function when handling zero‑weight parcels. The issue surfaced after the recent deployment of the `v2.4.1` release.
