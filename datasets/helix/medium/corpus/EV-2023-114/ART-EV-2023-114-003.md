Subject: Pivoting Freight Tracking to Microservices  

Hi Maya,

Once upon a time in code, we built the tracking module as a monolith. That architecture served us well until the recent TransLink escalation exposed latency and reliability bottlenecks. After reviewing the incident logs and customer feedback, I propose we pivot to a microservices architecture: split into Tracking, Notification, and Analytics services, all behind a shared API gateway.  

Let me walk you through the plan:  
- **Sprint 1**: Refactor Tracking service, expose REST endpoints.  
- **Sprint 2**: Build Notification and Analytics services, integrate with gateway.  
- **Phased rollout**: Deploy to TransLink first, monitor, then roll out company-wide.  

In the grand tapestry of systems, this change will reduce coupling and improve scalability. Every line has a story, and this story is about resilience.

Best,
