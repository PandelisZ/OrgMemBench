# Tier 1 Support SLA Review — 2023-05-10

**Attendees:** Nia Osei (Support Lead), Thomas Green (Legal Counsel), Maya Patel (Product Manager), Luis Ramirez (Engineering Lead)  
**Note‑taker:** Thomas Green  

## Agenda
- Review recent Tier 1 escalation and SLA performance  
- Discuss potential SLA adjustment  
- Outline next steps for policy update  

## Discussion
- **Nia Osei:** “We had a Tier 1 ticket that slipped past the 8‑hour window, which caused a customer to express dissatisfaction. That’s a clear risk of churn.”  
- **Maya Patel:** “From a product perspective, tightening the SLA could improve our CSAT scores, but we need to ensure the support team can realistically meet a shorter window.”  
- **Luis Ramirez:** “Engineering can provide tooling to auto‑assign high‑priority tickets, but we’ll need to adjust the workflow in Jira to reflect the new response target.”  
- **Thomas Green:** “Legally, we must document any SLA change formally and communicate it to all stakeholders. I propose we revise the Tier 1 SLA to a 4‑hour response time, effective 1 June 2023.”  
- **Nia Osei:** “That aligns with our goal to reduce churn. I’ll coordinate with the support ops team to prepare the transition.”  

## Decisions
- The Tier 1 support SLA will be revised to a 4‑hour response time, effective 1 June 2023.  
- The updated policy will be documented in Confluence and communicated to all support teams via the weekly stand‑up channel.
