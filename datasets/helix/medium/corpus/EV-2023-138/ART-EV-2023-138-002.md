# Redesigning Shipment Tracking — 2023-10-05

**Attendees:** Diego Alvarez, Daniel Kim, Maya Patel  
**Note-taker:** Daniel Kim

## Agenda

- Review TransLink escalation
- Evaluate polling vs real‑time options
- Decide on next steps for tracking module

## Discussion

- **Diego:** “The 15‑minute lag is killing our client’s ETA accuracy. We need a faster path.”  
- **Daniel:** “Let me walk you through the current polling flow: every 10 min a job hits the REST API, aggregates status, and pushes to the dashboard. It’s simple but the delay is unacceptable.”  
- **Maya:** “What about WebSockets? We’d push updates instantly, but that adds complexity to the backend.”  
- **Daniel:** “In the grand tapestry of systems, the trade‑off is clear: we can drop the scheduled job queue and simplify the architecture if we adopt a push model.”  
- **Diego:** “Agreed. The client’s complaint is the catalyst. We should move fast.”  
- **Maya:** “We’ll need to refactor the client SDK and update the API docs. Also, monitor for any burst traffic.”  
- **Daniel:** “Every line has a story; let’s keep the story short and clear. I’ll draft the design spec.”  

## Decisions

- Transition the shipment tracking module from polling REST API to a WebSocket‑based real‑time update system.  
- Remove the scheduled job queue from the backend architecture.
